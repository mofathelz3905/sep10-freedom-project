## Choosing a topic

The topic I decided to focus on for my Freedom Project is soccer. I chose soccer because [it is my favorite sports]. I’m interested in finding out how technology is changing the game, both for professional players and for people who play soccer at school or in their communities. Some technologies I want to learn more about include video assistant referees, goal-line technology, and tools that help players and coaches study performance.

I also considered the motivations behind why people play soccer, but the sport caught my attention in particular because some people enjoy it, while others play it to earn money. As I began thinking about my project, I wondered how these different aspects function, and whether they make soccer fairer, safer, or more exciting. I am also interested in exploring new approaches that might be used in soccer in the future.

For my early research, I read IFAB’s VAR Protocol. It helped me understand that VAR is used to review certain important decisions, but the referee remains responsible for the final decision. I also looked at IFAB’s explanation of goal-line technology, which explained that the system tells match officials whether the whole ball has crossed the goal line. One thing I found especially interesting was that goal-line technology must confirm its result to the officials immediately and automatically, within one second.
