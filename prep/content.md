## Part A: existing technology

##### Software

- **Google Reader** — Google Reader helped people follow new posts from blogs and websites in one place. Google shut it down in 2013. [Source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Reader)

- **Vine** — Vine was an app for recording and sharing short looping videos. Its app and website were shut down in 2017. [Source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vine_(service))

- **Inbox by Gmail** — Inbox was an email app by Google that organized messages into groups and let users snooze emails. Google discontinued it in 2019. [Source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inbox_by_Gmail)

- **Wunderlist** — Wunderlist was a task-list app for organizing reminders and to-do lists. Microsoft shut it down in 2020. [Source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wunderlist)

- **Quibi** — Quibi was a streaming service that offered short videos designed for phones. It launched in 2020 and shut down later that year. [Source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quibi)

- **Google Play Music** — Google Play Music let users listen to streamed music and upload their own music libraries. Google replaced it with YouTube Music in 2020. [Source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Play_Music)

- **Periscope** — Periscope was a live-streaming app where people could broadcast video from their phones. Twitter discontinued it in 2021. [Source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Periscope_( service))

- **Adobe Flash Player** — Flash Player was software used to run animations, games, and videos on websites. Adobe ended support for it in 2020. [Source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adobe_Flash_Player)

- **Microsoft Encarta** — Encarta was a digital encyclopedia available on computers. Microsoft discontinued it in 2009. [Source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Encarta)

- **MSN Messenger** — MSN Messenger was a computer program for instant messaging with friends. Microsoft discontinued it in 2013. [Source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSN_Messenger)

- **Google Hangouts** — Hangouts was a messaging and video-calling service from Google. Google retired it and moved users to other services in 2022. [Source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Hangouts)

- **Yahoo Messenger** — Yahoo Messenger let people send instant messages and make online calls. Yahoo shut it down in 2018. [Source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yahoo!_Messenger)

- **AIM (AOL Instant Messenger)** — AIM was a popular instant-messaging program. AOL shut it down in 2017. [Source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AIM_(software))

- **Google Stadia** — Stadia was a cloud-gaming service that streamed games over the internet. Google shut it down in 2023. [Source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Stadia)

- **Club Penguin** — Club Penguin was an online game and community where players used cartoon penguin characters. Disney shut down the original game in 2017. [Source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Club_Penguin)
